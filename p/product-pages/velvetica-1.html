Carbon-Neutral Sourcing
Spun in Biella, Italy using 60% virgin extra-fine merino wool (19.5 micron) and 40% certified cruelty-free baby alpaca. Loosely looped on a 7-gauge Japanese flat knitting loom to preserve yarn loft and breathability.
Designed for an architectural relaxed drape with dropped armholes and ribbed elongated cuffs. If you prefer a tailored silhouette, we recommend sizing down one full size.
Hand wash in cold water with pH-neutral wool detergent. Lay flat on a clean towel to dry. Do not tumble dry. Store folded with natural cedar blocks to prevent stretching.
