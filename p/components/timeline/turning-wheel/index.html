About us

Fifteen years of building bikes by hand

2011

A garage and two frames

Emma and James weld their first steel frames in a rented garage in Bristol. Both bikes go to friends, who ask for more.

2013

Our first workshop

We move into a small workshop near the harbour and hire our first frame builder. Forty bikes leave the door that year.

2015

The Wanderer city bike

A commuter with fenders, lights and a rack built in. It becomes our best-seller and still is today.

2017

Repairs for life

Every frame we have ever sold now comes with free lifetime frame repairs. A bike should outlast its trends.

2019

Riding abroad

First shipments to Germany and the Netherlands. The team grows to eighteen people.

2021

Electric, carefully

Our first e-bike, with a battery you can lift out and a frame that rides well even with the motor off.

2023

A workshop under the sun

We open a new workshop with rooftop solar panels that cover most of the power it uses.

2025

Ten thousand riders

Our 10,000th bike leaves the workshop. We start buying back used frames to refurbish and ride again.
