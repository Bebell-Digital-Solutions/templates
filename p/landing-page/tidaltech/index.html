Frequently Asked

When do I get my hardware?

Your hardware setup should arrive at your designated address at least 3 days before your official start date. If it hasn't arrived by then, please contact IT support immediately.

How do I access the learning modules?

Once you have your Tidal Tech email set up, you can log in to the learning portal using your SSO credentials. All assigned modules will be visible on your dashboard.

Who is my onboarding buddy?

Your manager will assign an onboarding buddy within your first week. They will reach out to you directly via Slack to set up an introductory coffee chat.

What is the dress code for the office?

We are a "dress for your day" company. Casual attire is perfectly fine for everyday work, but you may want to dress up slightly for client meetings or special events.
